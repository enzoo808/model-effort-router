# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) in short form.

## Our standard

Be respectful and keep the discussion about the work. Disagree with the data or the rule, not with the person.

Expected:

- Welcoming, patient and constructive feedback; accepting it gracefully too.
- Citing a source when you challenge a number (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)).
- Writing in English or Turkish; both are fine.

Not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks of any kind.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Spam, or anything else that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies to every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions and review comments.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break this code, and may block a repeat offender.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer [@enzoo808](https://github.com/enzoo808) through GitHub. Use a private [security advisory](https://github.com/enzoo808/model-effort-router/security/advisories/new) if the report must stay private. Reports are handled in confidence.
